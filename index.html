The through line

Good work starts with
curiosity. And people.

I'm a software builder and leader based in Jacksonville Beach, Florida. At Higher Pixels, my work brings together two things I care about: making useful software and helping the people who build it do their best work.

My background spans software development, technical leadership, and business. These days, that includes leading the technical team at Buzzsprout, helping podcasters get their ideas out into the world.
